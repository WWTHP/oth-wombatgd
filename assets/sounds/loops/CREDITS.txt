WombatGD diesel sound loops - credits

These loops are adapted from Creative Commons recordings on freesound.org.
Changes made: cut to a section, converted to 48 kHz mono, loudness-leveled
(dynaudnorm + loudnorm), and the ends crossfaded into a seamless loop.

idle.wav
  "Volkswagen Golf II 1.6 Diesel Engine Start Idle Stop Mono.wav" by Soundholder

idle_mb200d.wav
  "Mercedes Benz 200 D idling. Recorded inside" by YleArkisto

Measured from the recordings (used as the mod's default pitch references):
  idle.wav        ~834 RPM  (27.8 Hz firing, 4-cylinder)
  load.wav        ~1170 RPM (9.7 Hz full-cycle rate, 4-cylinder)
  load_high.wav   ~1460 RPM
  idle_mb200d.wav  weak signal; RPM not measurable (recorded inside the car)

om617_on_<rpm>.wav, om617_off_<rpm>.wav
  Procedurally synthesized by the WombatGD mod (tools/synth_om617.py): a model
  of a Mercedes OM617 inline-5 naturally aspirated diesel (firing order
  1-2-4-5-3, combustion knock, injector/valve ticks, exhaust and intake).
  No recorded audio is contained in these files. The tone was shaped with an
  EQ curve measured against a W124 300D idle recording on Wikimedia Commons
  (by Chill96, CC BY 3.0); only the averaged spectrum was used.
