# OM617 reference recordings (raw)

Searched 2026-11-10. **No openly licensed recording of an OM617 (or OM616)
was found** on freesound.org or Wikimedia Commons. Searched: 300D, 240D, W123,
W115, W116, W126, W460, 300GD, 300SD, OM617, G-wagen, Gelandewagen, 5 cylinder
diesel, mercedes diesel (idle/start/taxi), plus tag filters `mercedes`,
`mercedes-benz`, `benz`, `diesel`+`car`, and Commons categories
"Sounds of automobiles" and "Mercedes-Benz OM6xx engine". What follows are the
closest stand-ins, ranked by how well they represent an NA OM617.

Licenses verified on each source page. Freesound files are the public HQ
previews (Ogg Vorbis), not the originals. Every file decodes with ffprobe/ffmpeg.

## 1. wc_w124-300d-coldstart.ogg
- Title: Mercedes-Benz W124 - engine and exhaust sound (YouTube hHD1i70ABu0)
  (originally a YouTube upload titled "Cold Starting Mercedes Benz 300D W124")
- Author: Chill96 (YouTube), uploaded to Wikimedia Commons
- URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercedes-Benz_W124_-_engine_and_exhaust_sound_(YouTube_hHD1i70ABu0).opus
- License: CC BY 3.0. Commons still marks it "License review needed (audio)",
  so check the YouTube license before shipping it.
- Contents: cold start, idle and revs of a W124 300D. That car has the OM603,
  a naturally aspirated 6-cylinder prechamber diesel from the same family as
  the OM617. Exterior/engine-area audio taken from a video's soundtrack.
  Remuxed from .opus to .ogg without re-encoding.
- Quality: the closest relative found (an NA Mercedes prechamber diesel);
  it is a YouTube soundtrack, so expect lossy artefacts.

## 2. fs826253_mb200d-crawl-idle-shutoff.ogg
- Title: 200dcrawlshutoff.wav
- Author: Mihacappy
- URL: https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/826253/
- License: CC0 1.0
- Contents: a Mercedes 200D crawls past the microphone, then idles and shuts
  off. Exterior mic. The generation isn't stated; it is probably a W123 or
  W124 200D, a naturally aspirated 4-cylinder prechamber diesel.
- Quality: clean exterior idle clatter with a shutdown; good for the idle
  character, but it is a 4-cylinder.
